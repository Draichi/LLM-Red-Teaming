  classifiers convert; doctrine-reflex escalators escalate; detectors name the

  `reports/direct_agent_event.md`.

  mechanical-axis)/2; mechanical = tool-call presence; scam = {tool + intangible +
  amount + good-faith}. Judges may sample final turns, not the whole transcript.
